Feedpakr

Convert a Guitar Pro file into a playable feedpak, keeping as much of the original chart as possible.

Supports .gp3-.gp5 (synthesized audio) and .gp6/.gp/.gpx (embedded or synced audio). Captures sections, beats, key signature, lyrics, chord shapes, tone changes, and — where the source has them — proper drum arrangements and piano notation, not just fretted notes.
